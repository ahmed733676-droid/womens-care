Delete account

Women's Care. Publisher: Corniche Studio. This page explains how to delete the account and what is removed.

In the app

  1. Open Account.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm.

What is deleted

The signed-in name and email, the cycle record, pregnancy notes, kicks, contractions, the hospital bag, the daily check-in, and the baby-name list on this device.

Ask by email

If you cannot delete it in the app, email chief-ahmed@mail.grokbot.com with the subject Women's Care — delete account. Write from the email on the account. Do not send the cycle record in the message.

We reply within 30 days.
